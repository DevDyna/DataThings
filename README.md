# Giant ore veins
A huge amount of ores that spawn neat to other

The spawn location is based on original ore block spawning ignoring any biome condition

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:ore_vein/overworld` :

- Coal
- Copper
- Iron
- Gold
- Emerald
- Redstone
- Diamond

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:ore_vein/nether` :

- Quartz
- Gold (nether gold)
- Ancient Debris


# Unique Thematic Caves

### Frost Caves
A new cave *biome* full of snow and ice

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:spawn_feat_snowy`

### Arid Caves
A new cave *biome* full of sandstone and sand

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:spawn_feat_desert`

### Wet Caves
A new cave *biome* full of mud , dirts and a few patch of vegetation

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:spawn_feat_swamp`

# Miner Station
A new structure useful early game to obtain some good gears and stuff

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:spawn_treasure`

# Sparse Grass Patches

Some random patch of dirt and grass underground with some plants that could be useful when you are stuck underground without food

**Spawn location:** `#wildcaves:spawn_sparse_grass`
